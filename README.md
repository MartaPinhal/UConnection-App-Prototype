# UConnection Porto

**Requirements Engineering & UX Project**

UConnection Porto is an academic team project developed during the **MSc in Information Science at FEUP**, as part of the **Information Systems Requirements Engineering** course.

The project explored how a digital platform could improve communication, connection and access to relevant academic and social information within the university student community.

## Project Overview

The project followed a structured requirements engineering process, moving from problem discovery and user research to requirements definition, prototyping and user validation.

The objective was to understand the challenges experienced by university students and translate those needs into structured system requirements and a practical digital solution.

## User Research & Problem Discovery

An initial Google Forms study gathered **36 valid responses** from university students.

The research explored student experiences, needs and difficulties, particularly around communication, integration and access to useful information within the university environment.

The findings informed the definition and development of the UConnection Porto concept.

The discovery process included:

- User research
- Personas
- Customer Journey Mapping
- Value Proposition Canvas
- Problem and context analysis
- Solution ideation

## Requirements Engineering

User needs and research findings were translated into structured system requirements.

The requirements engineering process included:

- Requirements elicitation and analysis
- User scenarios
- Use cases
- Business rules
- Preconditions and postconditions
- Functional requirements
- Non-functional requirements
- Requirements negotiation and conflict resolution
- Conceptual modelling

This process helped bridge identified user needs with the expected behaviour and structure of the proposed digital system.

## Proposed Solution

UConnection Porto was designed as a centralised digital platform for university students.

The proposed solution included functionality such as:

- Student matching and connections
- One-to-one messaging
- Interest-based groups
- University and social events
- Event registration
- Resource and information sharing
- Student feed
- Notifications

The project also considered alternative solutions, scalability, implementation risks and future development through a roadmap.

## User Validation

A follow-up study gathered **20 valid responses** and allowed participants to explore the UConnection Porto prototype.

No participants reported difficulties navigating the prototype.

The majority rated the prototype positively in terms of **usability** and **usefulness**.

Feature feedback also helped identify which areas of the proposed solution generated the most interest among participants.
